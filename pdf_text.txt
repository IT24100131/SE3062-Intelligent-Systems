Search Algorithms in Pac-Man
Objective: Implement the classic uninformed and informed search algorithms — DFS,
BFS, Uniform Cost Search and A* — and design admissible, consistent heuristics for two
structured problems (finding all corners, and eating all the food dots).
This is a Group assignment for a group of 4 members. Students must use the existing
teams they have. Each group must complete the tasks together, and everyone in the group
must have a complete understanding of the entire solution. You will be evaluated
individually on your overall comprehension of the group's work, as well as your individual
version control contributions.
Download link: search.zip.
Marking Scheme (Total: 100 Marks — 10% of Final Grade)
The total assignment is graded out of 100 marks, broken down into four components:
Autograder Score (40 Marks): Group marks based on the autograder output for Q1-
Q7.
Group Report (10 Marks): Graded on structure, formatting, clarity, and completeness
of the requirements.
Individual Git Contribution (10 Marks): Assessed via report screenshots and
repository verification.
Individual Viva Evaluation (40 Marks): A rapid-fire individual assessment of your
understanding of the code and algorithms. (Split into 20 marks for Algorithm
Knowledge and 20 marks for Code Comprehension).

How search works here. Every algorithm follows the same graph-search skeleton; only
the fringe changes.
1. Put the start state on the fringe.
2. Pop a node from the fringe. If its state is the goal, return the path of actions that
reached it.
3. If its state has not been expanded before, mark it expanded and push each
successor (with the extended path, and accumulated cost where relevant) onto the
fringe.
4. Repeat until the goal is found or the fringe is empty (return failure).

8 marks · autograder q5

wall grid or the whole GameState in the state.
